The Architectural Reality: Why the Daily Status Stays Around 24% to 30%

The overall percentage compares your daily submitted applications against an aggregate maximum safety cap of 567 actions/day across all 13 pipelines. Submitting 140 to 170 tailored applications every day is an extraordinary real-world volume. The percentage hovers at 24%–30% because over 350 theoretical slots belong to channels that are either structurally limited, starved of leads, or encountering platform obstacles:

●Curated Feed (Not Open Board)

Instahyre (100 cap) does not have an open search catalog. It only shows 5–15 curated company matches matching your exact profile. Once you apply, 0 opportunities remain. 100 unused cap = 17.6% of the denominator.

●Tight Filters & DOM Volatility

LinkedIn Easy Apply (20 cap) filters require "Past 24h" + "Easy Apply" + city filters, which yields 0 matching postings on most search combinations. LinkedIn also throttles search queries after repeated checks.

●Feed Dependent Pipelines

Cold Email (25 cap) relies on LinkedIn Harvester finding recruiter posts with emails. With LinkedIn post search yielding 0 leads, cold email dispatches only manual dropzone tickets. Web Forms (25 cap) also awaits manual tickets.
